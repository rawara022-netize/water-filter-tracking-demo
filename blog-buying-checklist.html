SMART BUYING CHECKLIST

7 Things to Check Before Buying a Water Filter

A practical checklist to help you compare water filter options before making a decision.

Reading time: 4–6 minutes

Start with your water, not the product advertisement

Water filters have different capabilities. Before choosing one, identify your water source and the specific quality concerns you need to address. Use reliable local water information or appropriate testing when necessary.

1. Identify your water-quality needs

Check a public water-quality report or arrange suitable testing for a private water source. Do not assume that every water supply has the same issues.

2. Verify certifications and performance claims

Look for independent certification relevant to your concerns. Read the exact model's performance data and confirm which contaminants it is tested to reduce.

3. Compare filtration technology

Carbon, reverse osmosis, UV and ultrafiltration work differently. Select technology according to the identified issue and verified product capabilities.

4. Calculate the total cost

Compare the purchase price, installation, replacement cartridges, maintenance and other running costs—not just the initial price.

5. Check replacement filter availability

Find out how often filters need replacement, what replacements cost and whether compatible parts are easy to obtain.

6. Measure your space and check installation

Check dimensions, plumbing requirements, water pressure, electricity needs and the space available under or beside your sink.

7. Review maintenance and warranty

Read cleaning instructions, warranty terms, service availability and the manufacturer's recommended maintenance schedule.

Questions to ask before buying

  • What specific water-quality issue am I trying to address?
  • What evidence supports the product's performance claims?
  • What will replacement filters cost over time?
  • Can I install and maintain the system correctly?
  • Does the product suit my household's water usage?
Important: Clear appearance or good taste alone does not establish that water is safe. If you suspect a safety issue, follow local public-health advice and arrange appropriate testing.

Final thoughts

A careful comparison can help you avoid paying for features you do not need. Prioritise verified performance, suitability for your water, maintenance and total ownership cost.

Educational content only. Product requirements and water quality vary. Verify all claims with the manufacturer and relevant independent certification.
